# VOICE — nathanhealea.com
As-of: 2026-11-01 · Owner: Nathan Healea · Applies to: all user-facing strings

## Persona (1 line)
a public-sector engineer filing his own report: first person, plain declaratives, dates and places first, no selling.

## Adjectives → anti-adjectives
- plain — but never flat
- warm — but never folksy
- exact — but never bureaucratic

## Casing & mechanics
- Case: sentence case on every heading, label and button.
- Punctuation: no exclamation marks; zero em dashes; contractions allowed.
- Numerals: digits for counts and years; months as "Jan 2023"; ranges "2021–2022".

## Vocabulary
- BANNED: passionate, journey, rockstar, ninja, guru, crafting, solutions (as a noun for work), innovative, synergy, "I love", leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, delve, landscape (plus the global blacklist, copy-system §3)
- REQUIRED: "built" or "maintain" over "crafted"; name the employer and the place; "people who use it" over "users"

## Scope rule
voice lives in standfirsts, side notes, color-page statements, the 404 and the footer line; nav, buttons and link labels stay functional: "Experience", "Projects", "About", "Download résumé", "View on GitHub".

## Example pairs (ours vs. never-ours, same slot)
- Standfirst: "I build and maintain the systems a university runs on, and I make them work for everyone who has to use them." / NEVER "Passionate full-stack developer crafting seamless digital experiences."
- 404: "This page isn't in the record. Return to the home page." / NEVER "Oops! Looks like you're lost 🧭"
- Footer: "Published from Eugene, Oregon." / NEVER "Made with ❤️ and lots of coffee"
