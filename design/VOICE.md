# VOICE — nathanhealea.com
As-of: 2026-11-16 · Owner: Nathan Healea · Applies to: all user-facing strings

## Persona (1 line)
a colleague talking about their work over beers: first person, plain and specific, business casual with some personality, no selling.

## Adjectives → anti-adjectives
- plain — but never flat
- warm — but never folksy
- exact — but never bureaucratic
- relaxed — but never flippant

## Casing & mechanics
- Case: sentence case on every heading, label and button.
- Punctuation: no exclamation marks; zero em dashes; contractions encouraged.
- Numerals: digits for counts and years; months as "Jan 2023"; ranges "2021–2022".

## Vocabulary
- BANNED: passionate, journey, rockstar, ninja, guru, crafting, solutions (as a noun for work), innovative, synergy, "I love", leverage, utilize, robust, seamless, delve, landscape (plus the global blacklist, copy-system §3)
- REQUIRED: "built" or "maintain" over "crafted"; name the employer and the place; "people who use it" over "users"

## Scope rule
voice lives in ledes, project write-ups, the 404 and the footer line; nav, buttons and link labels stay functional: "Experience", "Projects", "About", "View on GitHub", "Switch to dark theme".

## Example pairs (ours vs. never-ours, same slot)
- Lede: "I build and maintain the systems a university runs on, and I make them work for everyone who has to use them." / NEVER "Passionate full-stack developer crafting seamless digital experiences."
- 404: "Nothing here. This page doesn't exist, or it moved when the site was rebuilt." / NEVER "Oops! Looks like you're lost 🧭"
- Footer: "Based in Eugene, Oregon." / NEVER "Made with ❤️ and lots of coffee"
